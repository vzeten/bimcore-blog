## FAQ

### How do I update the BIMCORE plugin?

1. If a prompt asks you to update the plugin, click **Yes** in that window.
2. If the **BIMCORE plugin update required** window appears, click **Download the latest version**.
3. Save your open projects, then close Revit.
4. Install the downloaded bimcore.msi file using [Install the plugin](/guides/bimcore-plugin/installation#3-install-the-plugin).
5. Start Revit.
6. Wait a few minutes for the sets to download again.

### What should I do if my purchased sets are missing?

1. On Revit's **Add-Ins** tab, click **Settings**.
2. In **User settings**, check that the email address shown matches the email address used for your order.
3. If the addresses differ, [sign in with the email address used for your order](/guides/bimcore-plugin/sign-in).
4. On Revit's **Add-Ins** tab, click **Panel**.
5. In the upper-right corner of the plugin window, click the refresh icon with the tooltip "Refresh information on the panel".

If you need help, email us at `vzeten@gmail.com`.

### What should I do when Windows displays "Windows protected your PC"?

1. In the window displaying "Windows protected your PC", click **More info**.
2. In the same window, click **Run anyway**, as shown in [Install the plugin](/guides/bimcore-plugin/installation#3-install-the-plugin).

### How do I reset my password?

1. On Revit's **Add-Ins** tab, click **Settings**.
2. In **User settings**, enter the email address you registered with and click **Forgot Password?**.
3. A new password arrives by email.
4. Open your email inbox.
5. Open the email containing your new password.
6. In **User settings**, enter the new password in **Password** and click **Sign in**. See [Reset your password](/guides/bimcore-plugin/sign-in#reset-your-password) for the full procedure.

### Which password should I use to sign in?

1. If you have already registered, enter the password from the "BIMCORE Revit Add-in" email in **Password** in **User settings**.
2. If you have not registered for the plugin, follow [Register and sign in](/guides/bimcore-plugin/sign-in).
