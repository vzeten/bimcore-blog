## FAQ

### How do I update the BIMCORE plugin?

If the **New Plugin Version** window appears, click **Yes**. If **BIMCORE plugin update required** appears, click **Download the latest version**.

Then close Revit and install the downloaded bimcore.msi file by following [Install the plugin](/guides/bimcore-plugin/installation#install-the-plugin).

If no update window appears, download the installer and reinstall the plugin by following [Install the BIMCORE plugin and open it in Revit](/guides/bimcore-plugin/installation).

### Why are the sets I purchased missing from the plugin?

On the **Add-Ins** tab, click **Settings**. In **User settings**, your email address appears under **You are logged in with email:**. Check that it matches the email address used for your order. To use a different email address, click **Sign out**, then sign in with the email address used for your order. See [Register and sign in to the BIMCORE plugin](/guides/bimcore-plugin/sign-in).

On the **Add-Ins** tab, click **Panel**. In the upper-right corner of the plugin panel, click the refresh icon with the tooltip **Refresh information on the panel**.

If your purchased sets are still missing after refreshing, email support at [mail@bimcore.one](mailto:mail@bimcore.one).

### How do I continue installation when Windows displays “Windows protected your PC”?

Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. See [Install the plugin](/guides/bimcore-plugin/installation#install-the-plugin) for screenshots.

### How do I sign in if I've forgotten my password?

In **User settings**, enter the email address you registered with and click **Forgot Password?**. See [Get a new password](/guides/bimcore-plugin/sign-in#get-a-new-password).

### Where can I find free families?

Free families are available in a separate set called **Free families**. The **Free families** set appears in the plugin panel after you sign in. See [Open a set's project and insert a family](/guides/bimcore-plugin/sets).

### Why doesn't my INT LINES store password work?

The plugin uses a separate password, which you receive by email after registering. Click **Register**, then sign in using the password from the email. See [Register and sign in to the BIMCORE plugin](/guides/bimcore-plugin/sign-in).
