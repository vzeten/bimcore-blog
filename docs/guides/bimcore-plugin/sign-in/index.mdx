The **User settings** window contains the **Register** and **Forgot Password?** links, the **Email** and **Password:** fields, and the **Sign in** button.

Your INT LINES store account password won't work in the plugin. The plugin sends you a separate password by email.

## Register

1. In Revit, click **Settings** on the **Add-Ins** tab.

   ![Settings button on the Add-Ins tab](./img-15.png)

2. In **User settings**, click **Register**.

   ![Register link in the User settings window](./img-13.png)

3. In the **Register** window, enter the email address you used for your purchase. If you haven't bought anything, enter any email address you use, then click **OK**.

   ![Register window with the Email field and OK button](./img-12.png)

You'll receive a password by email. Use that password to sign in to the plugin.

If the email hasn't arrived, check your spam folder.

## Sign in to the plugin

1. In **User settings**, enter the email address you registered with in **Email** and the password from the email in **Password:**, then click **Sign in**.

   ![Sign in button in the User settings window](./img-14.png)

2. After signing in, wait for the sets to download. This takes 1–2 minutes.

## Get a new password
1. In **User settings**, enter the email address you registered with in **Email** and click **Forgot Password?**. The plugin displays the message “New password has been sent to your email.”
2. Enter the new password from the email in **Password:** and click **Sign in**.

If you need help, email us at [mail@bimcore.one](mailto:mail@bimcore.one).
