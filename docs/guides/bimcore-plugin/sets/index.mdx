After you sign in, the plugin panel shows the INT LINES sets you've purchased. If your purchased sets are missing, sign in with the email address used for your order.

Free families are available in a separate set called **Free families**.

Below each set's name, you'll find an **Open** button and a family count, such as **12 families**. Click **Open** to open the set's project, or click the family count to expand the family list. Each family has an **Insert** button to place it in your own project.

## Open a set's project

In the plugin panel, click **Open** below the set you want to use.

![Sets tab in the BIMCORE plugin panel with the Open button and i icon](./img-07.png)

## Insert a family into your project

1. Open your project in Revit and open the view where you want to place the family.
2. In the plugin panel, click the family count below the set you want to use.

   ![Expanded set showing the family count and Insert buttons](./img-08.png)

3. Click **Insert** for the family you want to place.
4. Click where you want to place the family in the view.
5. Press **Esc** twice to finish placing families.
